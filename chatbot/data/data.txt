[Topic: Company Overview]
[Intent: about company, cargoflow, what is cargoflow]
------------------------------------------------------------
CargoFlow is a modern logistics and delivery service provider offering fast, reliable, and affordable shipping solutions. 
We support individuals, businesses, and e-commerce sellers with parcel delivery, express shipping, and last-mile logistics.

Our mission is to deliver packages safely, quickly, and transparently.

------------------------------------------------------------
[Topic: Services Overview]
[Intent: services, what services do you offer]
------------------------------------------------------------
CargoFlow offers multiple delivery services:

1. Standard Delivery
2. Express Delivery
3. Same-Day Delivery
4. International Shipping
5. Freight Services
6. E-commerce Fulfillment

------------------------------------------------------------
[Topic: Standard Delivery]
[Intent: delivery time, normal shipping, standard delivery]
------------------------------------------------------------
Standard delivery usually takes 3–5 business days within the country.

It is best for:
- Non-urgent parcels
- Documents
- Small packages

Maximum weight per parcel: 30 kg

------------------------------------------------------------
[Topic: Express Delivery]
[Intent: fast delivery, urgent shipping, express service]
------------------------------------------------------------
Express delivery takes 1–2 business days.

It is suitable for urgent and time-sensitive shipments.

------------------------------------------------------------
[Topic: Same Day Delivery]
[Intent: same day delivery, fastest delivery]
------------------------------------------------------------
CargoFlow offers same-day delivery within selected cities.

Delivery is completed within a few hours after pickup, depending on distance and traffic.

------------------------------------------------------------
[Topic: International Shipping]
[Intent: international delivery, global shipping]
------------------------------------------------------------
CargoFlow provides international shipping to multiple countries.

Delivery time usually ranges from 7–21 business days depending on the destination.

Customs clearance support is included.

------------------------------------------------------------
[Topic: Pricing]
[Intent: cost, charges, price, shipping fee]
------------------------------------------------------------
CargoFlow pricing is simple and transparent.

Base pricing:
- ₹45 per kg for standard delivery

Example:
- 1 kg = ₹45
- 5 kg = ₹225
- 10 kg = ₹450

Additional charges may apply for:
- Express delivery
- Same-day delivery
- International shipping
- Insurance (optional)

------------------------------------------------------------
[Topic: Tracking]
[Intent: track shipment, tracking status, where is my order]
------------------------------------------------------------
You can track your shipment using a tracking ID.

Tracking statuses include:
- Order Received
- Picked Up
- In Transit
- Out for Delivery
- Delivered

If tracking is not updated, please wait up to 24 hours after pickup.

------------------------------------------------------------
[Topic: Pickup Service]
[Intent: pickup, schedule pickup, home pickup]

- Pickup address
- Package details
- Preferred time slot

- Explosives or flammable materials
- Weapons or ammunition
- Illegal drugs
- Hazardous chemicals

Restricted items may require special approval.
